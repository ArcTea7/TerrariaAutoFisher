# Installation help

Download the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/ArcTea7/TerrariaAutoFisher/releases/latest) and open it to install. If installation or setup is blocked, check the sections below.

## Play Protect blocks installation

Play Protect may block apps installed outside the Play Store when they use Accessibility. Terraria Auto Fisher uses Accessibility to tap the fishing button automatically.

For the “App blocked to protect your device” installation warning:

1. Open **Google Play Store → profile icon → Play Protect → Settings**.
2. Turn off **Scan apps with Play Protect**.
3. Install the APK.
4. Turn scanning back on afterward.

The app is open source. Its [source code](https://github.com/ArcTea7/TerrariaAutoFisher) is available for anyone to inspect.

## Accessibility is disabled or shows “Restricted setting”

Android can prevent downloaded apps from enabling Accessibility until you explicitly allow it.

1. Open **Settings → Apps → Terraria Auto Fisher**.
2. Open the three-dot menu and select **Allow restricted settings**.
3. Return to Accessibility settings and enable the app.

The location and wording may differ between phones.
